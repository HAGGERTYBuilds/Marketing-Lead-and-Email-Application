Storm Prep and Emergency Loss Checklist

For property managers, owners and homeowners. Work through the first section before the rain. Keep the rest where your team can find it fast.

Emergency? A person answers 24/7209-323-6200

Part 1

Before the storm

  • Clear gutters, downspouts and roof drains. Make sure downspouts carry water away from the foundation.
  • Walk the roof and flashing. Look for lifted shingles, open seams and clogged scuppers on flat roofs.
  • Check grading and storm drains around the building and parking areas. Clear debris from inlets.
  • Test sump pumps and any backup power they rely on.
  • Find and label your shutoffs: main water valve, gas, and electrical panel. Make sure more than one person knows where they are.
  • Photograph the property now, inside and out, so you have a before record for any claim.
  • Pull your insurance details and keep the policy number and your agent's contact with this sheet.
  • Move valuables, files and inventory off the floor in basements, storage rooms and ground floor units.
  • Save 209-323-6200 in your phone as RHINO Restores, and share it with tenants, staff or your maintenance team.

Part 2

The first hour after water gets in

  • Stay safe first. Keep people out of standing water near outlets or equipment. Shut off power to affected areas only if you can reach the panel safely.
  • Stop the source if you can: close the main water valve for a pipe or fixture leak.
  • Call RHINO Restores at 209-323-6200. We can tarp, board up and start extraction the same day.
  • Photograph and video everything before anything is moved or thrown out: the source, standing water, damaged materials and contents.
  • Move what you can to a dry area: electronics, documents, rugs and furniture. Put foil or blocks under furniture legs that stay put.
  • Skip the shop vac on large losses and never use a household vacuum on standing water.
  • Notify your insurance carrier and write down the claim number.

Part 3

The first 48 hours

  • Get drying started fast. Mold can begin to grow on wet materials within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Keep a log of calls with your carrier and adjuster, with names, dates and what was agreed.
  • Keep every receipt for emergency purchases, temporary housing and anything replaced.
  • Don't throw out damaged items until your adjuster has seen them or approved disposal.
  • Ask for the moisture readings. RHINO keeps daily moisture logs and pulls equipment once readings show dry.
  • Plan the rebuild with the same team. RHINO handles mitigation and reconstruction and can work directly with your insurance carrier.

This checklist is general guidance. Follow your carrier's instructions and local safety officials during any emergency.
